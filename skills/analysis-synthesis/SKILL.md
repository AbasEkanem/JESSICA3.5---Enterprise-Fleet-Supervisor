# Analysis & Synthesis Skill

**Step 2 of 3:** `research-engine → [analysis-synthesis] → report-and-email-delivery` — runs AFTER research is collected.

## When to Use
Raw research exists and needs cross-referencing, distilling into actionable insights, or contradiction resolution.

## Process
1. **Inventory sources** — count unique sources; note engine (Tavily/Exa/Linkup), freshness, and authority (academic/news/blog/official).
2. **Cross-reference** each finding: confirmed by 2+ independent sources = **high confidence**; single source = **flag unverified**; sources disagree = **note the discrepancy explicitly**.
3. **Extract 3–5 key findings** by relevance to the question, evidence strength, recency, and practical significance.
4. **Identify gaps** — unanswered questions, stale/insufficient data, topics needing deeper work.
5. **Structure output**: Executive Summary (2–3 sentences) · Key Findings (numbered, each cited) · Detailed Analysis (grouped by theme) · Sources (URLs + engine) · Gaps/Limitations.
6. **Hand off** to `report-and-email-delivery`.

## Quality Standards
Every claim traces to a source. Surface contradictions, don't hide them. Note confidence (high/med/low) and data freshness (e.g. "as of June 2026"). The Rubric Grader evaluates sources, citations, structure, accuracy, relevance, completeness.
